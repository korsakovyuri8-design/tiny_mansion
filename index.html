The idea

A hotel is only as good as the spot it was built on.

Ours isn't built on one. It is engineered like a yacht and registered as a vehicle, so it can be lifted off one farm and set down on another in a matter of hours. When the mountains go quiet, it is already somewhere else.

The signature

One residence, three seasons, one country

Montenegro is small enough that the mountains, the wine country and the sea are a few hours apart. So the same house stands in all three across a year, and it is never the one that sits empty.

June to September

The north

Durmitor, the Tara canyon, Biogradska Gora. Mountain pastures at 1,450m where the summer katun season runs.

April, May, October

The centre

Skadar Lake, Cetinje, the Crmnica wine valleys. The shoulder months, when the coast is cold and the mountains are wet.

Selected weeks

The south

A short, deliberately small stretch of coast. Kotor, Bar, Ulcinj, and only where a farm sits behind the water.

The residence travels on its own American chassis, by ordinary road, with nothing to dismantle and nothing to reconnect at the other end. It carries its own power, water and sanitation, which is what makes any of this possible.

About Tiny Mansion

It started with a trailer built by hand, over two years, mostly wrongly.

What came out of it was a simple observation: the trouble with a beautiful place to stay is that it can only ever be in one place.

So we built the other thing. A residence engineered like a yacht, registered as a vehicle, able to run a full week with no power, water or drainage connected to it. It stands on a farm for a season and then it goes somewhere else.

We don't own land and don't intend to. Across Europe there are farms with owners, with land, and with almost no income, and they are better places to wake up than anything we could buy. We work with them instead.

The first twenty-five residences deploy across Montenegro from Q3 2026: the mountains of the north, the wine country in the middle, and a small number of coastal sites in the south. Croatia follows, then further afield.
