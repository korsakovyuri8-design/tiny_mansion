What the residence does not do

It does not give you a residence permit. That permit requires cadastral real estate with a tax-assessed value of at least {{PERMIT_THRESHOLD}}, and our house is registered as movable property. We would rather say so here than let you find out later.

Worth knowing about the apartment route too: a property-based permit does not allow you to work or run a business in Montenegro, and those years do not count toward permanent residence.

What does work

Residence through your own Montenegrin company. You hold the company, the company holds the management arrangement for your house, and you are employed as its director. Those years count in full toward permanent residence, and you may legally operate.

It has a running cost. To renew, the company must have paid at least {{PERMIT_TAX_MIN}} in taxes and contributions over the previous year. Citizens of the EU, EEA and Switzerland are exempt from that requirement.
