Plan B

If the country stops working, the house leaves the country

A weak location has a second exit, and it costs about €6,000 a unit. An apartment has no such exit: it can only be sold, and in a weak market that means a discount plus the agent plus the taxes.

The non-EU neighbours

Serbia, Bosnia, Albania, North Macedonia. Towing, CEFTA customs, a new site and fresh listings — about €6,000 a unit, and a matter of days. The first candidate is the Albanian coast: outside the EU, with a growing premium segment.

Into the EU — honestly, and dearer

Croatia, Slovenia, Greece, Italy. Here the destination country adds import VAT unless a temporary-admission regime applies. That is the single question deciding whether Plan B extends into the EU, and we are checking it with a customs adviser now.
