Stated: 18% a year on the shares, and an average annual return of 30.2% in the optimistic case, 23.0% in the moderate one. The footnotes say what that is built from: the property's value is assumed to rise 10%, 7% and 5% a year over the next five, and income from reselling the shares is counted inside the average annual return.

We are not claiming anyone is misleading a buyer. We are taking published figures and laying them out. Demand the same from anyone who shows you an annual yield as a single line. Including from us.

How it works

Three climates, one asset

Montenegro's seasons peak at different times. The residence follows demand instead of waiting for it.

North

Durmitor, Tara, Kolašin

The mountain season, running after the coast has closed. Canyons and national parks, with almost no competition at the premium end.

Centre

Lake Skadar, Crmnica, Lovćen

Wine country with a steady flow all year. It carries the shoulder season, when neither the mountains nor the sea are working.

South

Bay of Kotor, Bar, Ulcinj

The Adriatic summer peak. We go in selectively, and only where a hotel could not be built in the first place.

  1. Booking opens before placement

    The season starts with confirmed dates rather than cold demand. We see where the bookings concentrate before we physically move the asset.

  2. The residence goes to where those bookings are

    The decision follows the data: occupancy under a threshold, service quality on site, the turn of a season, a concentration of demand, a partner leaving.

  3. The move takes hours

    The country is compact; a run between zones is tens of kilometres, a few hundred at most. Transport is in the operating budget as a routine line, not an event.

  4. Nothing spent on land

    Residences stand on working farms. The site fee is zero or nominal: the farmer gets guests and a sales channel, we get the site. Eight farms are already under signed framework agreements.

Classic development has one answer to a weak location: cut the price. A mobile asset has a second one — stop standing in it. That is the only real difference; everything else is finish.

The comparison is structural: it is about a type of asset, not a particular building or developer. The other side of it is in "Where we are weaker": an apartment has a resale market and land underneath it, and we have neither.

The mechanics

Come and stay, or let the asset work

Income is calculated across the whole network at once, not from "your" house. A bad season at one site does not sink you personally: the risk is spread across everyone in the pool.

You come

You stay on the Adriatic in a stone house. For plenty of people that is the reason to be here at all.

You pick the location to suit the mood: the bay, the lake or the mountains. The residence is waiting, ready.

Nights don't expire and can be moved within the year. You can hand them to friends or family.

You don't come

The residence takes guests, the revenue goes into the shared pool, and you take your share of it.

There is nothing for you to do: bookings, cleaning, meeting guests, repairs — all of it sits with the operator.

You see everything in your account: occupancy, revenue, and your share, in real time.

Three residences in three climate zones: when it rains on the coast, the mountains are in season. That is protection against seasonality built into the structure, not promised in words.

Exit

Three routes, all named in advance

  1. Bought out at scale-up

    Once the model is proven, the next stage raises larger capital, and that capital buys out the early participants. You take back what you put in, plus what it earned. The early ones go first, in order of entry.

  2. Roll into the next stage

    If you would rather not take the money out, you carry your participation into the scaled project on priority terms. The full contribution counts, and the status and privileges you have accumulated stay with you.

  3. Stay in the pool

    If you are happy with the nights and the income, you stay as you are, for as long as the network runs. No forced exit and no fixed term.

The return of the principal is tied to the project succeeding and the next capital arriving. This is a route out, not a date in the calendar: we are not promising when, we are showing the mechanism. Until it fires, you are in an income-producing pool with personal nights.
