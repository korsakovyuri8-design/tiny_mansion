The problem · 01

One country, one September,
a thirtyfold difference

Montenegro takes over a billion euro in tourism revenue a year and almost all of it lands in four months, on one strip by the sea. The landscape that would carry the rest of the year is already there, and it is empty.

89.7%
Nights on the coast

September 2025. The country effectively runs on a single strip of shoreline.

2.9%
Nights in the mountains

Durmitor, Biogradska Gora, Komovi. World-class landscape, almost no guests.

€1.3bn+
Tourism revenue, 2025

A record year: over 2.7m arrivals and more than 15m overnight stays.

A hotel is fixed to the ground it was built on. It has to pick one of those two halves and live on that half's season — and when the location weakens there is exactly one lever left, which is price.

The problem · 02

The land is not the constraint.
The building is.

Across the north and the centre there are working farms with owners, with land and with almost no income from it. None of them can put accommodation on that land.

Capital
A guest building is a construction project before it is a business.
Drainage
A septic tank means excavation, an approval and a permanent position.
Services
Power and water have to be run to a place that was chosen for its remoteness.
Permission
A building permit on agricultural land, on a timescale no farm can wait out.

So the best places to wake up in this country stay empty, and the people who own them keep farming.

A partner yard. Nothing on it was built for us.

04
Innovation

Autonomy is a consequence of components,
not a word in a brochure

Every line below is the reason the residence can stand at the edge of a farm with nothing run to it. Take one out and the asset becomes a building again.

Incinerating toilet
No septic tank — and with it no excavation, no approval and no fixed position. This is the single component that makes the site fee zero.
4 × LiFePO₄ 200 Ah ≈ 38 kWh
7 × 400 W solar ≈ 2.8 kW peak
A week of every system running with no connection and no generator, recharged in daylight. No fuel, and no one delivering it.
Orbital shower, ≈90% recirculation
Water delivered once a week instead of daily, which is what makes a remote pitch operable at all.
Dual-zone heat pump, R-40 envelope
Works from −10 to +40 °C, so the same asset earns in a mountain winter and an Adriatic August.
Cerbo GX telemetry · Nuki access
Charge, water and temperature visible remotely; check-in with no staff on site. A call-out follows the data rather than a guest's phone call.

This is the production specification, agreed with the manufacturer. It has not been built yet: the 2023 prototype was made by hand to a different one.

07
Target groups

Four customers, and what each one is actually buying

SEGMENT 01

The guest

European traveller, 2–4 people, €280–320 a night. Wants the landscape rather than a resort, and will not accept a compromise on the bathroom to get it. Books direct or through the usual channels.

SEGMENT 02

The farm

A working household with land and no capital. Pays nothing and builds nothing. Gets guests who arrive, eat, buy and book what the farm already offers, and a sales channel for its own produce.

SEGMENT 03

The investor

The house itself, €168,000–€188,000 all in. Buys the residence outright in their own name — movable property with a serial number and an invoice, not cadastral real estate — and we run the guests under a management contract they can end. Our fee is subordinated to a 12% preferred return: below it we are cut first.

SEGMENT 04

The venue

An established EU bar or coffee brand. Buys a mobile unit under its own livery to reach events, festivals and private bookings its fixed premises cannot.

Segments 01 and 02 are two sides of the same placement: the guest is why the farm says yes, and the farm is why the guest comes. Segment 03 funds the first season. Segment 04 is a separate market that does not depend on any of the other three.

The model

The asset follows the demand
instead of waiting for it

Booking opens first
The season starts with confirmed dates rather than cold demand. We see where the bookings concentrate before the asset moves.
The residence goes there
The decision follows data: occupancy under a threshold, the turn of a season, a concentration of demand, a partner leaving.
Revenue pools
Income is calculated across the whole network at once, not from one house. A bad season at one site is spread across the pool.
Three zones, three seasons
  • North — Durmitor, Tara, Kolašin. The mountain season, running after the coast has closed.
  • Centre — Skadar, Crmnica, Lovćen. Wine country, a steady flow that carries the shoulder season.
  • South — Kotor, Bar, Ulcinj. The Adriatic peak, entered selectively and only where a hotel could not be built.

Classic development has one answer to a weak location: cut the price. A mobile asset has a second — stop standing in it.

Where we actually are

What exists today, and what does not

Done
  • Eight farms under signed framework agreements, across the north, the centre and the south.
  • A confirmed manufacturer and a production specification agreed with them, component by component.
  • A prototype, built by hand in 2023, and six years of work on the model behind it.
  • Company registered in Montenegro: TinyArc Group d.o.o., Bar.
  • Two commercial units specified and costed, with the financial model published in full.
  • A bilingual site live at tinymansion.co, and a separate investor document in two languages.
Not yet
  • No unit built to the production specification. The prototype was made to a different one.
  • No night sold, no guest, no euro of revenue. We would rather say it here than have it found.
  • No European homologation yet for the commercial line; the claims on the site were softened until it exists.
  • Rates for the commercial line come from two North American operators and want checking against European ones.

At a stage where everyone has promises, an honest calculation is the only thing that carries weight.
